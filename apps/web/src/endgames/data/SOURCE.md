# Endgame family texts (`familyTexts.ts`)

The short endgame descriptions in `familyTexts.ts` (M32-4, ADR-025 section 5) were written for
DaisyChess in our own words and are GPL-3.0-or-later like the rest of `apps/web`. No text is
copied or adapted from Wikipedia, lichess, chess.com, books or any other source. The keys are the
`EndgameFamily` values of `@daisychess/endgames` (one entry per family, including `other`).

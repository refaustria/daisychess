5. Shared pool rules

  1. Pool access requires a pool token issued by an operator or the administrator. Pool tokens expire by default and have limits: concurrent sessions, search size and a daily engine-time budget (30 minutes by default).
  2. Spot-checks: the hub re-runs a small, random sample of pool searches on its own engines and compares the result with the node's answer, to catch broken or dishonest nodes. It uses only the position already sent to the node.
  3. A node whose answers repeatedly disagree is suspended from the pool for 24 hours; after repeated episodes it is banned from the pool. Private tokens of its operator keep working. Only the administrator lifts a pool ban.
  4. The administrator may disable nodes, tokens or operators at any time, in particular for abuse, security reasons, wrong results, privacy breaches or inactivity, and may close their connections immediately. We aim to give a reason on request, but need not give prior notice.
  5. Pool capacity is shared fairly and can be full (“no capacity”); there is no entitlement to a slot.

What you can do

Everything you need between two games.

From a quick look at the eval bar to a full review with a guided tour — without leaving the board.

Analyse with Stockfish

Stockfish 19 runs in your browser — or faster on a server. Eval bar, best-move arrows and up to five lines at once.

Automatic review

Accuracy, key moments and a guided tour through your best moves and mistakes — plus what players at your level usually play.

Openings trainer

Search about 3,900 named openings, drill them with spaced repetition, then play the position out against Maia or Stockfish.

Play your way

Stockfish with clocks and premoves, human-like Maia from 1100 to 1900, a friend via a link, or your live lichess games.

Import in a click

Fetch your recent lichess and chess.com games — clock times included — or paste any PGN, and review straight away.

Explore openings

The opening name as you play, plus lichess masters and database statistics for every position you reach.

Puzzles

300,000 puzzles. Daily, rush, and your own mistakes.

Train tactics with the public lichess puzzle database, sorted by difficulty and theme. A built-in starter set works offline, and on a phone the board stays put while you solve.

Explore puzzles →
  • The daily puzzle, a new one every day
  • Puzzle Rush: solve as many as you can against the clock
  • Puzzles from the tactics you missed in your own games
  • Filter by theme or opening, with hints when you are stuck

Game review

Learn from the game you just played.

Finish a game and the review is already running. DaisyChess points out where the game turned, shows the better move with its line, and walks you through it.

  • Accuracy for both players and an evaluation graph
  • Time spent per move and mistakes made in time trouble
  • Where you left the opening book, and what the book move was
  • Reviews are saved, so a game opens instantly next time

Community nodes

Lend your engine. Help a player.

Got a spare computer, a home server or a Raspberry Pi? Run a DaisyChess node and lend its engines to friends — or to everyone through the shared pool. It only connects out: no open port, no domain, no certificate.

Run a node → Operator portal
  • Private node — only people you give a token can use it.
  • Pool node — share part of your machine with strangers.
  • Fair by design — the hub spot-checks pool results.
  • One command — Docker on amd64 or arm64.
